## The concept

A tool cell is the unit the bus is organized around: a kit plus a captain who
knows it. The kit travels as a set, bits marked, manuals linked, packed to
roll. The captain is one person who knows what is in the kit, how it packs,
and who to ask when something needs care.

Splitting the bus into cells is a proposal, not a fixed rule: one workable
way to organize, open to change by the people who use it.

Every cell gets a printable sheet that lives in the kit: the inventory as a
checklist, the care rules, and who to ask. Check the kit against its sheet
every time it goes out and comes back.
