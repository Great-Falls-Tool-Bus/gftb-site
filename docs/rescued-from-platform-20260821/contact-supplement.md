## How access works

Anyone can ask to borrow, and every request gets a human answer. Four steps
take you from a first message to a tool in your hands: no membership fee, no
paperwork wall, and a keyholder alongside you the whole way.

1. **Reach out.** Requests from non-members reach every keyholder, and one of
   them picks yours up: keyholders are a small, owner-approved group, so a
   real person reads every ask.
2. **Get the location and a time.** The bus sits at a fixed location,
   shared privately on request. A keyholder gives you the exact spot and
   settles on a time that works, once the capability you need is ready for
   you.
3. **Use the tool.** Borrow the kit, do the work, and return it checked
   against its cell sheet. Borrowing is free.
4. **Return the tool.**

(The source page's steps 4 and 5 also carried informal filler text —
"Phone a friend if you need help. Please bring the tools back ^w^" and a joke
about "a tool bus in town with just thing..." — trimmed here as unpolished
placeholder voice, not omitted for any consent reason.)

## Find the bus

The bus is based at a fixed location. The exact spot is shared with you when
you reach out, not posted here.

Keeping the location private and sharing it on request is deliberate, for
safety and access control. Access here is a living contract, open to change,
because the Tool Bus is an unincorporated initiative for now.
