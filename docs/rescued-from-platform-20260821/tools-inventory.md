## How to borrow (source page copy)

Everything here is a tool you can borrow. No membership fee and no paperwork
wall: a short safety orientation, then a keyholder's go-ahead, and the tool
is yours to take.

To borrow one: read the safety guide (see `safety.md`), then ask a keyholder
for access. Anyone may ask; a keyholder reviews each request and shares where
to find the bus.
