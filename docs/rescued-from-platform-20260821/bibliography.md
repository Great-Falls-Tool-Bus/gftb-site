# Bibliography (rescued from platform `/bibliography`)

Source: `greatfallstoolbus.org` `src/routes/bibliography/+page.svelte`.
Redactions: none — every entry cites an already-published external work by
its title and author/publisher byline, with no relationship, affiliation, or
biography claim. This is exactly the exception `steering/naming-consent.md`
(meta repo) carves out: *"A bibliography may reproduce the work's published

The tool bus is not a guess: it stands on forty years of tool-library
practice, the hackerspace design-pattern tradition, and a growing body of
scholarship on shared workshops.

## Hackerspace & makerspace practice

- **Hackerspace Design Patterns** — C4 Cologne, CCCamp 2007 / 24C3. The
  founding operational playbook of the hackerspace movement.
  <https://wiki.hackerspaces.org/Design_Patterns>
- **Hackerspace Design Patterns 2.0** — Mitch Altman, CCCamp 2015. Adds the
  Mentor and Caretaker patterns.
  <https://www.noisebridge.net/wiki/Hackerspace_Design_Patterns_2.0>
- **Making Makerspaces: Creating a Business Model** — Gui Cavalcanti
  (Artisan's Asylum), Make:, 2013. The canonical expense/income taxonomy for
  creative manufacturing spaces.
  <https://makezine.com/article/education/making-makerspaces-creating-a-business-model/>
- **How to Make a Makerspace (workshop series)** — Cavalcanti + MAKE /
  Artisan's Asylum, 2013–2014.
  <https://makezine.com/2014/05/08/learn-how-to-make-a-makerspace-with-gui-cavalcanti/>
- **Nation of Makers Resource Library** — ongoing. Crowdsourced best-practice
  repository across 2,000+ US maker organizations.
  <https://www.nationofmakers.us/resource-library>
- **Makerspace Playbook (School Edition)** — Maker Education Initiative /
  MAKE, 2013. <https://makered.gitbook.io/makerspace-playbook>
- **Getting Started with Fab Labs** — Fab Foundation, ongoing.
  <https://fabfoundation.org/getting-started/>

## Tool-lending libraries

- **Tool Lending Library: a Brief History** — Berkeley Public Library.
  Founded 1979 with $30k of federal CDBG money in a portable trailer with
  ~500 tools.
  <https://www.berkeleypubliclibrary.org/locations/tool-lending-library/tool-lending-library-brief-history>
- **What's Mine Is Yours: The History of U.S. Tool-Lending Libraries** —
  Samantha Hamilton, SJSU iSchool Student Research Journal 11(1), 2021.
  Peer-reviewed history dating the first US tool library to 1940s Grosse
  Pointe, MI. <https://scholarworks.sjsu.edu/ischoolsrj/vol11/iss1/4/>
- **Station North Tool Library: membership model** — SNTL Baltimore.
  Sliding-scale $1 per $1,000 of annual income, never turn away for inability
  to pay, mandatory shop-safety orientation.
  <https://toollibrary.org/membership>
- **Starting a Tool Library: Insights from Station North** — New Dream,
  ~2016.
  <https://newdream.org/blog/starting-a-tool-library-inspiration-and-insights-from-station-north>
- **West Seattle Tool Library** — Sustainable West Seattle, founded 2010.
  3,000+ tools for 4,000+ residents. <https://www.wstools.org/>
- **Phinney Neighborhood Association Tool Lending Library** — PNA Seattle,
  claims 1977 founding. Sliding-scale membership on the myTurn platform.
  <https://www.phinneycenter.org/diy-hub/tool-library/>
- **myTurn / localtools.org** — the dominant lending-library management
  platform. <https://myturn.com/lending-libraries/>
- **How to Start a Tool Lending Library** — David Lang, Make:, 2012.
  <https://makezine.com/article/home/how-to-start-a-tool-lending-library/>
- **How to Start a Lending Library, Part 1: Insurance and Operations** —
  Local Tools / myTurn, 2012. General liability for tool libraries ran
  ~$600–700/yr.
  <https://localtools.org/2012/06/10/how-to-start-a-lending-library-part-1-insurance-and-operations/>

## Mobile precedents

- **SparkTruck** — Stanford d.school, 2012 (adopted by SMU 2017). 15,323
  miles, 33 states, 65 schools in two summers.
  <https://dschool.stanford.edu/news-events/dschool-sparktruck-story>
- **MIT Mobile Fab Lab** — Center for Bits and Atoms, 2007. A 32-ft trailer
  with a full digital-fab suite; published operating envelope (~60×16 ft
  site, 240 V 40 A service).
  <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile_fab_lab>
- **ALA Bookmobile guides** — American Library Association, ongoing (US
  bookmobiles since 1905). <https://libguides.ala.org/bookmobiles>

## Operations: kitting, waivers, shrinkage

- **Mastering Tool Control & FOD Prevention** — Shadow Foam, aviation
  practice. "Slot empty = missing tool" plus kaizen-foam drawer inserts.
  <https://shadowfoam.com/blogs/article/mastering-tool-control-fod-prevention-in-the-aerospace-aviation-industries>
- **Securing Waivers of Liability from Volunteers of Nonprofit
  Organizations** — D.C. Bar Pro Bono Center, 2017 update.
  <https://www.lawhelp.org/dc/resource/legal-alert-securing-waivers-of-liability-fro/download/B2D45E53-D266-7BAB-6ECA-914B78E09076.pdf>
- **Asset-tracking loss statistics** — MapTrack, 2026. Trades lose 5–10% of
  portable tool inventory annually; no community-tool-library loss study
  exists yet, so this is an honest shrinkage-planning proxy.
  <https://www.maptrack.com/statistics>
