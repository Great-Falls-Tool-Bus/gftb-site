## Tools the kits need

The current tool gap list is the "Wants" rows in `tools-inventory.md` (iron +
ironing table, serger). Kept once there, not duplicated here.

## Hands and skills

Not every want is a tool. The bus needs people too:

- **Help getting the bus situated.** Hands to move it, park it, and outfit
  the inside.
- **A graphic designer.** A logo, some graphics, a palette that holds up.
- **Site reviewers and writers.** Sharp eyes for the prose, and a hand
  writing the manifesto: read a page and say what's soft, or draft the words
  that are missing.

## How a want gets filled

A tool-want comes off the list the same way anything joins a kit: someone
donates a tool that passes the donation criteria (see `donate.md`) and a cell
captain checks it in. A hands-or-skills want comes off the moment someone
reaches out and starts.
