# Donate a tool (rescued from platform `/donate`)

Source: `greatfallstoolbus.org` `src/routes/donate/+page.svelte`.
Redactions: none.

Tools are what the bus needs most — the ones that are hard to own alone:
complex, large, heavy, or blessed with many little bits and bobs. Give one
outright, or lend it and stay its owner; either way it goes to work.

## Give it, or lend it: your call

Donating outright is the simplest path, and for a tool you're done with, the
best one. But you don't have to give up ownership to put a tool to work: you
can make a tool available to the bus and stay its owner — lend it, let a cell
run it, and take it back whenever you need it. For a big or expensive tool,
lending is often the right fit. Given or lent, a tool goes through the same
three questions below and the same keyholder hand-off.

## Before you hand one over, walk it through three questions

- **Can it travel?** The bus has limited space, and tools still need to
  travel safely when borrowed or relocated. Tools that pack into a cart,
  case, or chest work best. If your tool needs one, donating (or building)
  the kit alongside it is half the gift.
- **Are the bits marked?** Chisels, collets, blades, jigs, chargers: mark
  every piece so it visibly belongs to its set. Engraver, paint pen, or tag —
  anything durable counts.
- **Can it be repaired, used, and maintained?** Note the model number. If you
  know where the manual or datasheet lives, link it. Note who to reach out to
  when it needs care.

## Arrange the hand-off

Ready to hand off a tool (for good or on loan), or want to talk through
whether yours is a fit? Reach a keyholder. That's the whole process.
