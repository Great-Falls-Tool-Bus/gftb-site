## What we are

The tool bus is a shared tool library on wheels, and it stands on forty years
of tool-lending-library practice and the hackerspace design-pattern
tradition (see `bibliography.md` in this same directory).

The Great Falls Tool Bus is an unincorporated community project: there is no
nonprofit entity and no fiscal sponsor behind it yet. Because of that,
nothing on this site is a charitable solicitation and no donation is
tax-deductible.

> Source comment, preserved: "OPERATOR-CONFIRM: tax status = unincorporated /
> no 501(c)(3) / no fiscal sponsor; no tax-deductible claims. Recommended
> default, not operator-confirmed."

## What we believe

- **Shared, not sold.** Serious tools are expensive to own and easy to
  under-use. Pooling them puts capable equipment in more hands for far less
  money, and keeps good tools working instead of gathering dust.
- **Equity-forward.** The bus is meant to lower the barrier to making things.
  There is no membership fee, and nobody is turned away for inability to pay.

## Access, in one sentence

Anyone may ask to borrow: a short safety orientation and a keyholder's yes
are the whole gate, and donations are welcome but never required.

> Source comment, preserved: "Access model (ratified): keyholders are a
> curated, owner-approved group; anyone may request access; non-member
> requests reach all keyholders."
